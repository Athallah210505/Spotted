# Dish photos from Google reviews

Sixteen captioned dish photos for twelve matched venues. Source photo URLs, author names and contributor links, source venue URLs and capture times are retained in `src/features/catalog/data/google-places.json` under `menuPhotos`.

These are visitor photos and dish captions, not an official priced menu. No generated food images or inferred prices are included. Two visually ambiguous dish captions were excluded after inspection. This records provenance rather than blanket ownership of third-party media.
